3. Features

Academic/skills features: cgpa, ssc_percentage, hsc_percentage, aptitude_score, projects, internships, certifications, backlogs.

Socioeconomic features: household_income_band, parental_education, internet_access.

Audit-only (never predictors): gender.

Excluded identifiers/high-cardinality: student_id.

4. Preprocessing and statistical methods

Numeric: median imputation (+ standardisation for scale-sensitive models). Categorical: most-frequent imputation + one-hot.

All preprocessing sits inside scikit-learn Pipelines, so it is fit on training folds only (no leakage).

Statistical tests: Welch t-test or Mann–Whitney U (numeric); chi-square or Fisher exact (categorical); effect sizes (Cohen's d, rank-biserial r, Cramér's V); Benjamini–Hochberg correction; Wilson CIs; multivariable logistic regression (odds ratios). Association is not causation.

5. Model training methodology

Stratified 80/20 train/test split (seed 42); train n=1200, test n=300.

Selection: 5-fold CV ROC-AUC on training split. Selected model: Logistic Regression.

Probability calibration: sigmoid (Platt) via 5-fold CalibratedClassifierCV on the training split.

Models trained: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Extra Trees, Gradient Boosting, HistGradientBoosting, Support Vector Machine, Decision Tree. Unavailable (optional libs not installed): XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost.

8. Limitations

Socioeconomic coverage is limited to the columns actually present in the data.

Predictions are probabilities, not guarantees. Fairness has been checked only on audit columns available.
